Default mobile voice Mari is speaker 0, not the server or Piper speaker mapping.

Modification record: FastSpeech2's two FlexRandomUniform nodes are lowered to built-in
RANDOM_UNIFORM by scripts/lower-tts-random.py. All buffers, learned tensors, quantization,
and input/output contracts are preserved. The paired hifigan-est.v2.tflite is unmodified.
Originals are retained outside packaged assets in models/upstream. See the bundled
voices/et-neurokone-v1/manifest.json for original/derived SHA-256 hashes and exact URLs.

Native Processor.java is ported under MIT. Changes: package/class names, removal of
private-text logging, explicit UTF-8/Locale.ROOT, API-24-compatible join, safe segmentation,
unsupported-character/number limits, lone-digit and negative-sign guards, and preservation
of words surrounding numeric ranges and zero currency quantities. No EstNLTK, Flutter runtime, Unidecode, speech-service
app, system voice or network frontend is required. All text processing is local.

The recording dataset's CC-BY-4.0 statement is separate from the published MIT grant for
these model files and code. No dataset recordings are included. Generated review samples
are not a phrase dictionary or application fallback. Voice quality requires listening review.
