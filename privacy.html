Privacy policy

My Little Voice is an Android communication app for children and adults. No account is needed. There are no ads or analytics in the app. Family communication content is processed on the device; it is not sent to the developer through the app. Google Play delivery, chosen exports, support contact and website visits involve the separate processing described below.

On your device

Boards, wording, silent buttons, schedules, drafts, settings, Parent passwords and recovery copies use local app storage. Selected photos and audio are imported locally; microphone access is used when you choose to record. Speech runs locally after its voice is installed, including after restart. Supertonic audio may be reused in bounded memory, isolated by language, voice, text and speech settings. Estonian and legacy English bypass that cache. Process termination releases it; it is not included in family backups. Interface, communication and document languages are independent and do not translate saved family text. Voice preparation also keeps language, download outcome and short error-stage codes on this device for recovery after interruption. These local records are updated by later voice actions, contain no family wording or personal media, are excluded from family backups, and are not sent to the developer.

Google Play voice delivery

English Supertonic is delivered with initial installation. Estonian has an independent optional Play download for future installations. During the transition, retained Estonian compatibility assets also accompany the app, including fresh installations; eligible existing installations create a verified private copy without changing voice settings or family content. Other supported voices download independently through Google Play Asset Delivery in setup or protected Language & voice. Sizes, combined totals, storage needs and possible mobile-data charges are shown before confirmation. Play handles network preferences and asset updates and processes device metadata and app version for delivery, separately from family speech content. Google’s terms and privacy practices apply; the developer does not control its retention.

Voice integrity, terms acceptance and activation are checked locally. Acceptance is saved on this device. External voice downloads and voice-file import are unavailable in this Play flow. Downloaded, verified working voices remain offline. Failed downloads or verification do not replace a working voice. Compatible older voices, including the earlier English fallback where needed, remain during migration until a replacement succeeds.

Storage and deletion

English installation assets and transitional Estonian compatibility assets remain part of the app even after a working voice is removed. The private working copy is separate. Other Play source archives and working files may both remain after activation. Temporary copies are cleaned after success or a later startup. Recovery generations and legacy shared files remain while needed. Removal deletes that language’s working files and requests removal of its optional Play module; physical reclamation is not guaranteed immediately. Optional reactivation may require another download; included assets can be prepared offline. Removing a voice does not reset family content. Clearing app data or uninstalling can erase local content. Automatic Android backup is disabled. Exported files must be deleted separately.

Backups, sharing and printing

Backups can contain personal wording, photos, recordings and settings. Restore imports them locally. Exporting a backup or PDF, sharing a file, or using Android printing sends the selected content to your chosen destination. A cloud folder, messaging app or network printer may process it under its own terms. Voice archives and in-memory speech caches are not family backup content. Keep a separate backup you control. Private temporary backup copies are used for native saving and sharing. Save/cancel/failure copies are cleaned when the operation ends. Shared copies remain available to the chosen receiving app and are cleaned at the next app start or export after they are 24 hours old; Android may evict its cache earlier. Closing the share menu does not confirm delivery.
