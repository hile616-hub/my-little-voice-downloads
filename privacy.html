Privacy policy

When you tap a picture or type a message, your device makes the voice. The developer cannot listen in or read your boards. My Little Voice has no account, ads or tracking. Your family’s words, pictures and recordings stay in the app unless you choose to save or share a copy elsewhere.

On your device

The app keeps boards, photos, recordings, schedules, drafts and settings on this device. An editable drawing can still contain a photo you traced or stamped over. The keyboard learns words and short phrases from completed or spoken messages. You can clear them for each language in Parent. Learned words stay out of portable backups.

Microphone settings

Your microphone is used only when you choose to record a sound for a button. The clip stays on this device and can be included in a family backup. Speaking with pictures or typing works with the microphone off. Tap Stop and save recording, leave the recording screen, or wait for the recording limit to stop the microphone.

Android Settings → Apps → My Little Voice → Permissions → Microphone → Don’t allow. You can change this again. Saved recordings remain.

You choose which picture, recording or backup to import. You can cancel the file chooser. The app does not need access to your whole library. A cloud folder you select handles its part of the transfer.

Google Play voice delivery

Google Play uses device information and the app version to deliver the app and optional voices. Your words and recordings are never part of a voice download. Installed voices work offline. You can skip extra voices or use Wi-Fi.

Android lets download components check connectivity, run and finish a transfer, briefly keep the device awake and receive a restart event. An internal permission protects messages between app components. These system permissions require no separate microphone-style prompt. Voice choices, applicable terms acceptance and short download or error records stay on your device.

Storage and deletion

Android’s automatic app backup is disabled. Removing a button can leave its picture or recording in the library. A Parent reset makes a local recovery copy. To erase private app data, including recovery copies, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall it. Delete exported backups, PDFs and shared copies separately. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase your boards.

Backups, sharing and printing

A family backup can contain personal words, photos, editable drawings, recordings and settings. A PDF can contain the pictures and words you choose to print. A file you save, share or print goes to the destination you pick. Anyone with access to an exported backup may be able to read it. Restoring it imports the contents locally. After sharing a backup, the private temporary copy is cleaned up on the next app start or export once it is 24 hours old. Recipients and cloud or printing services manage their own copies.
